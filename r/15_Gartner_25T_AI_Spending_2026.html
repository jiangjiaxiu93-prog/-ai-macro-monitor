Gartner: Worldwide AI Spending Will Total $2.5 Trillion in 2026

Main Prediction

Gartner projects global AI spending will reach $2.52 trillion in 2026, representing a 44% year-over-year increase.

Spending Breakdown

Infrastructure Dominance - **AI infrastructure: $1.366 trillion in 2026** — more than half of total AI spending - Infrastructure construction alone accounts for $401 billion in additional spending during 2026 - Represents approximately 17% of total AI expenditures

Server Growth - **AI-optimized servers +49%** - Represent 17% of total AI spending in 2026 - Data center spending +31.7% → surpassing $650 billion in 2026 (up from nearly $500 billion)

GenAI - GenAI model spending +80.8% growth expected - Software market share rises by 1.8% in 2026

Key Strategic Shift

"AI investments will increasingly be driven by organizational maturity and proven business value, rather than visionary pilot projects." Organizations are moving away from experimental initiatives toward demonstrable ROI.

Market Maturity Assessment

Gartner characterizes AI as currently in the "Trough of Disillusionment" — the stage before reaching the "Plateau of Productivity" on its hype cycle. The firm expects AI solutions will increasingly be distributed through established software vendors rather than risky standalone ventures.

Lovelock's Key Quote

"Because AI is in the Trough of Disillusionment throughout 2026, it will most often be sold to enterprises by their incumbent software provider rather than bought as part of a new moonshot project. The improved predictability of ROI must occur before AI can truly be scaled up by the enterprise."

Three Primary Forces Reshaping Procurement

1. Procurement as Operating System: Budgeting becomes governance-driven rather than innovation-focused 2. ROI Predictability as Gateway: Funding expands fastest where measurable outcomes align with spending cycles 3. Incumbent Distribution Advantage: Bundling across cloud, data, security, and enterprise applications creates vendor concentration

Scaling Outlook

Broader organizational scaling of AI will accelerate once return on investment becomes more predictable and measurable across larger enterprises.
